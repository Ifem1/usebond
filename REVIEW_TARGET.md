# Review target

This document is not a score prediction. It records what must be proven for a strong review under the GenLayer project criteria.

## GenLayer fit

Evidence to demonstrate:

- licence language is genuinely semantic rather than reducible to a fixed boolean lookup;
- rights holder and user have conflicting incentives, so neither should unilaterally interpret the frozen terms after the use is known;
- independent consensus changes a consequential outcome: whether a permission credential exists;
- ambiguous terms fail safely as `INCONCLUSIVE`;
- the app does not claim GenLayer decides external law or ownership validity.

Potential reviewer challenge to answer with the product itself: "Why not a centralized lawyer or ordinary LLM?" The response should be visible in the trust model: immutable inputs, independent validator reconstruction, no single operator deciding, and a consensus result controlling credential issuance.

## Contract quality

Evidence to demonstrate:

- three contracts have distinct responsibilities rather than artificial file splitting;
- terms and intent commitments are deterministic;
- the validator independently interprets material clauses and obligations;
- output-shape validation alone cannot approve the leader result;
- finalization controls permit issuance;
- access control and replay protection are tested;
- all error/uncertainty paths are deliberate.

## Engineering

Evidence to demonstrate:

- clean install and documented commands;
- current-toolchain lint, schema and direct-mode proof;
- deployment manifest and exact-source verification;
- fee estimation before writes;
- wallet/network failure handling;
- accepted vs finalized lifecycle observer;
- CI and production build;
- reproducible live flows.

## Frontend / UX

Evidence to demonstrate:

- rights registry, licence folio, Use Composer, Permission Lens and Permission Passport each have a clear purpose;
- exact intended use is understandable before signing;
- material clauses and conditions are readable after assessment;
- provisional assessment cannot be confused with issued permission;
- public permit can be verified without connecting a wallet;
- responsive and keyboard-accessible production app.
